About GPT-6.1 Terra — and how this calculator works

GPT-6.1 Terra is OpenAI's mid-tier GPT-6.1 model, released October 26, 2026: $1.00 input / $5.00 output per million tokens, a 1.05M-token context window and up to 128K output. It sits between GPT-6.1 Sol (flagship reasoning, exactly double the price) and GPT-6 Luna (budget tier, one tenth of the price); its predecessor GPT-5.6 Terra cost $2 / $12.

Method. The comparison table runs the same workload (your inputs above) through every model at Standard rates. Cache writes and the 272K long-context surcharge are Terra-specific and are counted in Terra's own total above, but excluded from the comparison table so the four models stay on equal footing.

Who Terra fits. If your project is a support agent, RAG chatbot or document pipeline with a repeated system prompt, Terra's $0.05 cached reads usually make it the cheapest capable tier. If your project is mostly simple classification or FAQ replies, Luna does it at a tenth of the price. If your project lives or dies on the hardest reasoning steps, price Sol for those steps and Terra for everything else.

FAQ

How much does GPT-6.1 Terra cost per million tokens?

$1.00 input, $0.05 cached input, $5.00 output. Cache writes are $1.25 (30-minute retention) or $2.50 (12-hour) per million tokens.

What is the 272K surcharge?

Any single request whose prompt exceeds 272,000 input tokens is billed at 2× input and cache rates and 1.5× output — for the entire request, not just the excess.

Is Terra cheaper than GPT-5.6 Terra for cache-heavy agents?

Usually by a lot: cached reads fell from $0.20 to $0.05 per million. On a 10M-input session at 97% cache hit with 200K output, Terra costs about $1.79 versus $4.94 on GPT-5.6 Terra.

Do Batch and Flex really halve the bill?

Yes — every rate halves ($0.50 input, $2.50 output). If your workload tolerates asynchronous processing, it is the single biggest saving available.

How do I estimate my monthly GPT-6.1 Terra bill?

Multiply your monthly input tokens by $1.00 and your output tokens by $5.00, per million, then adjust for caching and mode. The calculator above does this in one click; the $9.99 planner pack adds 25 ready-made workload budgets.

What does cached input cost on GPT-6.1 Terra?

Cached input reads cost $0.05 per million tokens — 20 times cheaper than fresh input at $1.00. Writing the cache costs $1.25 per million tokens for 30-minute retention or $2.50 for 12-hour retention.

Is GPT-6.1 Terra cheaper than GPT-6.1 Sol?

Yes, exactly half. Terra is $1.00 input and $5.00 output per million tokens; Sol is $2.00 input and $10.00 output per million.

How does GPT-6 Luna compare to GPT-6.1 Terra?

Luna is the budget tier at $0.10 input and $0.50 output per million tokens — one tenth of Terra's price — aimed at high-volume, simpler tasks. Terra is the mid tier for work that needs stronger reasoning.

What does Fast mode cost on GPT-6.1 Terra?

Fast mode costs double the standard rates: $2.00 per million input tokens and $10.00 per million output tokens. Batch and Flex modes go the other way at half price.

Does the 272K surcharge also raise output prices?

Yes. When one request's prompt exceeds 272K tokens, input and cache rates double for the whole request and output rises 1.5 times, from $5.00 to $7.50 per million tokens.

Which GPT-6.1 tier should a support chatbot use?

Most support chatbots fit Terra: strong answers at $1/$5 per million with cheap cached reads for repeated system prompts. Luna suits simple FAQ replies at one tenth of the price; Sol is for the hardest reasoning steps.
